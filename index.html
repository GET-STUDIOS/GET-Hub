📖 GET Survival Field Manual

Practical survival knowledge, emergency procedures, bushcraft and family preparedness.

📚 Quick Index

💧Water Purification & Storage+
  • Water priority: Safe drinking water should be treated as an immediate priority during prolonged disruptions.
  • Boiling: Bring clear water to a rolling boil. Allow it to cool in a clean covered container.
  • Filtration: Use a purpose-built water filter whenever possible. Know what the filter is designed to remove.
  • Chemical treatment: Follow the manufacturer's dosage and contact time exactly.
  • Storage: Use clean food-safe containers and keep stored water away from sunlight and contamination.
  • Redundancy: Maintain more than one purification method.
🔥Fire Making+
  • Tinder: Prepare several dry, easily ignited materials before attempting ignition.
  • Kindling: Collect progressively larger dry material before lighting the fire.
  • Fuel: Use suitable untreated dry wood.
  • Ignition: Carry at least two independent ignition systems.
  • Airflow: Build the fire so oxygen can reach the flame.
  • Safety: Clear combustible material around the fire and extinguish it completely before leaving.
⛺Emergency Shelter & Insulation+
  • Location: Avoid flood channels, dead trees, unstable slopes and rockfall areas.
  • Weather: Position the shelter to reduce wind and rain exposure.
  • Ground insulation: Insulate underneath the body to reduce heat loss.
  • Tarps: A tarp can provide fast overhead and wind protection.
  • Dryness: Keep sleeping equipment dry whenever possible.

GET Field Manual: This manual is a preparedness reference and does not replace professional medical, emergency or technical training. Follow official local emergency instructions whenever available.
